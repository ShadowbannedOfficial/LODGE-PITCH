What comes next.

Optional phases of the 12-month strategy. Scoped and priced separately after the initial 90 days, and decided together at each review. Not part of this contract.

Days 91–180 / Build out the reel channel

A character-led, serialized workplace show, told through our host, the manager, depicting everything he has to deal with, from rich clientele to stripper drama. Each reel is an approved episode with a beginning, a problem and a payoff. Everyone on camera is cast and signs a release; real guests stay private. Food and the look move to feed posts and Stories. Scoped and priced at the Day-90 review.

Days 181–270 / Clipping

The modern-day TV ad.

The new frontier in media buys. Standout moments from the series, spread across a network of accounts that guarantee millions of views. A separate contract, scoped at the Day-180 review.

At each review

Decide together whether to expand.

A note on the format. Amalfi Jets is an outside example of a serialized workplace show, not a Chalant client. The private-jet charter company turned its founder, staff and clients into a TikTok reality show. Revenue went from about $3 million before TikTok to on track for $120 million in 2026 (Kolin Jones, Skift, April 2026). Their first TikTok drew 1 million views, sent 100,000 people to the website and brought in about 10,000 flight requests in a week (Skift). As of November 17, 2026, @amalfijets had 2.95 million TikTok followers and 171 million likes. The Lodge has a better stage for the same format: eccentric clientele, employee drama, and a manager worth watching (we have some great ideas for the host).

A measured approach.

Organic-first. No paid ads or boosts.

The vision is a 12-month organic, no-ad content strategy. The only commitment locked in is the 90-day initial agreement. This plan is organic only.

Platform-conscious, tasteful content.

Every guest on camera signs a release. No nudity, no dance prices, no solicitation language, no money-throwing, and nothing that breaks Meta, TikTok or YouTube rules. The Lodge’s restraint is also what protects reach: platforms hide racy content from new viewers.

Weekly email updates, and a full Day-90 review.

Results are evaluated against starting metrics, not invented guarantees. Before any contract, Chalant will provide a full statement of work and a baseline audit of The Lodge’s current social media.
